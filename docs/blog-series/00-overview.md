# Blog series: Agentic Engineering, learned in public

Five posts. Each is standalone-valuable, builds on the previous, and has a **pre-writing checklist** — things you must build or verify before publishing that post. The checklists are the learning mechanism: you can't write post 3 until you've run a real loop, and you can't write post 4 until you've drawn one real edge.

`docs/blog-draft.md` is the raw monolith — most of it feeds posts 1–2. Cannibalize freely.

## Series rules

1. **One claim per post, proven with your own machine.** Every post has a personal artifact: a log line, a before/after, a score table. This is what separates it from the 10,000-word slop the skeptics [17] predicted.
2. **Publish in order; the checklists gate you.** If a checklist item fails, that's the post — "I tried X and it broke" is better material than success.
3. **Each post links back to the series index and forward to the next.** Post 5 is the compounding payoff; it only lands if 2–4 are real.
4. **Voice: practitioner, first person, short paragraphs.** Show commands and log excerpts. No "in today's fast-paced world."
